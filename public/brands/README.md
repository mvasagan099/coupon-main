# Catalogue logo assets

These brand marks identify the sample brand catalogue. Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. The samples do not imply a partnership, an official coupon, or authorization to redeem an offer.

Locally bundled assets, with no runtime logo CDN dependency:

- Facebook, Google Play, Apple, Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, Swiggy, Zomato, Uber and Starbucks: Simple Icons 16.35.0, `icons/*.svg`, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.35.0/icons .
- Amazon and Flipkart: Simple Icons 11.15.0, `icons/amazon.svg` and `icons/flipkart.svg`, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons .
- Meesho: https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/517730/meesho (CC0, as listed on the asset page).
- AJIO: https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/504079/ajio (CC0, as listed on the asset page).
- Myntra: https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/518072/myntra (CC0, as listed on the asset page).

The two Simple Icons license files are included alongside these assets. All SVG artwork is bundled unmodified; presentation sizes and backgrounds are applied by the application CSS.
